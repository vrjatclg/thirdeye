MISSION CHALLENGE & INNOVATION

The Thermal Imaging Challenge

Standard vision models trained on daytime RGB imagery fail in combat. Thermal and night-vision environments introduce unique computer vision hurdles that ThirdEye solves.

⚠

Thermal Blooming & Halos

Flares, hot engine exhausts, and muzzle flashes over-saturate IR detectors, blinding conventional thresholders and creating false positives.

THIRDEYE: Adaptive Dynamic Range Normalization
👁

Zero Chromatic Texture

Thermal FLIR lacks RGB color channels and surface textures. Detectors must rely strictly on subtle gradient differentials and structural heat signatures.

THIRDEYE: CLAHE Local Contrast Enhancement
🎯

Sub-Pixel Distant Targets

High-altitude reconnaissance drones capture tanks and soldiers as tiny 12×12 pixel blobs. Standard image downscaling destroys these critical signatures completely.

THIRDEYE: SAHI Sliced Window Hyper-Inference
🔄

Classification Flicker & Occlusion

Thermal smoke, treelines, and aspect angles cause frame-by-frame label flipping and multiple false target counts for a single enemy asset.

THIRDEYE: ByteTrack + Temporal Track Voting
